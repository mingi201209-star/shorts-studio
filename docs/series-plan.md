Two rules: (1) a big, readable event in the first second (crash, spin, blow-out); (2) dense, fast content (a fact or a number on every
shot) keeps and replays the viewers who stay.

## Format (every episode)
- 38-42 s, 13 short lines at +15 % speaking rate, a new shot every 2-4 s.
- Opening: labelled split (green / red), test conditions, live numbers from the model; the event happens before 1.0 s.
- Middle: the hidden mechanism (detailed parts, cutaways, lab shots) with fact cards; real-world numbers from textbook formulas or
  standard guidance, never invented statistics.
- End: one piece of advice, then a loop back into the first frame.
- Title pattern: "Why ... ?" or "Same X, Only One ..." + emoji. Every Short goes into a playlist named "One Difference".
- Pinned comment on each upload: "Which difference should we test next?" (engagement and ideas).
- The stylised driver (EP.08) everywhere a person appears.
