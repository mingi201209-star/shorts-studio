## Description
Every day, roads catch things that are completely out of control: a car crossing into oncoming traffic, a twenty-ton boulder, even a jet that couldn't stop in time. Stopping them is easy. Stopping them without hurting anyone is the hard part. Here are seven clever ways engineers do it, and the one trick almost all of them share.
