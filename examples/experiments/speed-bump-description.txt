Same height, very different jolt. Why does a short speed bump hit so much harder than a long speed hump?

Look inside the suspension: the wheel has to follow the road. A short bump throws it up in a split second, a long hump lifts it gently, and the spring and damper have far less time to soak up the short one.

This is an illustrative computer simulation with assumed values (a typical passenger car, 7.5 cm bump height, simplified suspension), not a measurement of a real car or a safety statement. The picture is a 3D animation.
