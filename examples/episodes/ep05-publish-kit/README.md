## Description
Your seatbelt is built to give way on purpose. In many modern cars a pretensioner first pulls the belt tight, then a twisting metal bar in the reel (the load limiter) lets out a little webbing, so your chest gets a longer, gentler stop. Same idea as a crumple zone: a longer stop means a smaller peak force.

This is a simplified physics model made for explanation, not a real crash test. It shows one belt type and no airbag; real cars differ and the numbers here are not from any real vehicle. Always wear your seatbelt.
