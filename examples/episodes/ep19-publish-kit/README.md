## Description
Why don't police spike strips make tires explode? Because a blowout at 60 could flip the car. So every spike is a hollow steel tube with a sharp tip. As the tire rolls across, the tubes punch into the tread, snap off the strip and stay stuck in the rubber. Air leaks out through each tube like breathing through a straw, and the tire goes flat in about half a minute instead of bursting. So the car slows down on its own, still steering straight.
