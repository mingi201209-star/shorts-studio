## Description
A car hits a wall, and we slow that tenth of a second way down to watch it.

The front crumples like an accordion. Those hidden crush rails absorb the crash energy over a longer stop, while the cabin keeps its whole shape. Then a rigid car hits the same wall at the same speed: it stops almost instantly and bounces back, and the cabin is not protected the same way.
